Why we're doing this

Notice the overgrown gardens around Durham? Older people have the space, students have the energy, and nobody is bringing them together.

Elderly cannot manage them, and students have no space. Both are missing out on fresh food, exercise, companionship and learning from each other.

Rooted Together is a community allotment hub plus a Garden Share scheme. Alongside it we run a free seed library, a small harvest market at the end of each crop cycle, and a festival at the end of the season.

How it works

If you have a garden

  1. Tell us about your gardenIts size, roughly where it is, and what help you'd like.
  2. We find you a growerOur team suggests someone nearby and introduces you in person.
  3. Enjoy it againYour garden is looked after, and you share the food and the company.

If you want to grow

  1. Sign up as a growerTell us what you'd like to grow, your experience, and when you're free.
  2. Meet your garden ownerOur team arranges the first meeting and comes along.
  3. Grow and log itLook after the space, record what you grow, and bring the extra to market.
Your safety comes first. We never show anyone's address or phone number on this site or in the app. Every first introduction is arranged by the Rooted Together team.

Seed library

Borrow seeds, grow them, bring some back

The seed library is a shared box of seeds anyone can use for free. Take what you need for the season. When your plants go to seed, save a few and return them so the library keeps growing.
